## Learning approach

1. Understand each concept and its purpose.
2. Compare concepts that are easy to confuse.
3. Connect threats to their impact and appropriate controls.
4. Test understanding with the self-assessment.

These notes are an independent technical learning resource. They are not a substitute for current standards, vendor documentation, or applicable legal requirements.
